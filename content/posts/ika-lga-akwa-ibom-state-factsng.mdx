Ika Local Government Area holds a distinct place within Akwa Ibom's administrative landscape. Nestled in our state, this LGA serves as an important hub for its communities, with Urua Inyang functioning as its administrative center. The region is characterized by its strong cultural identity, with the Annang people forming the backbone of its population and social fabric.
