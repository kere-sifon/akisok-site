There's something stirring in the Akwa Ibom diaspora community. Recently, diaspora experts came together with a clear mission: to help develop the state's economy. The gathering carried a powerful message captured in its theme—one about spreading Akwa Ibom values across the world and recognizing the potential we carry wherever we are.

What struck me most was the emphasis on what holds us together: unity, cooperation, and self-reliance. It's easy to feel disconnected when you're abroad, surrounded by different cultures and opportunities. But this conversation reminds us that our roots matter, and our collective strength—both here and there—can drive real change back home.

The diaspora has long been Akwa Ibom's hidden asset. We send money home, yes, but there's so much more we could be offering: skills, networks, ideas, and professional expertise. When experts from our community commit to developing the state's economy, they're not just talking about investments. They're talking about knowledge transfer, mentorship, and creating pathways for younger generations.

What resonates most is the push for self-reliance. We shouldn't be waiting for external solutions. We have the talent, the knowledge, and the determination within our own people. This movement acknowledges that and channels it toward something productive.

For those of us scattered across the globe, it's a call to remember that our success abroad is intertwined with the progress of home. We have something to give back—and initiatives like these create the structures to make it happen.
