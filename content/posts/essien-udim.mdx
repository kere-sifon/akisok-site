Essien Udim holds a special place in Akwa Ibom's geographical and cultural landscape. As one of the state's Annang-speaking Local Government Areas, it represents an important hub of our people's heritage and identity. Spanning approximately 298 square kilometers, the LGA encompasses diverse communities bound by shared language and traditions that have endured through generations.

The 2022 census recorded a population of around 246,600 residents, reflecting the area's significance as both a home for those who have stayed and a touchstone for the diaspora. For those of us scattered across the world, Essien Udim remains more than just a statistic—it's the place where many of our family roots run deep, where ancestral connections persist, and where the Annang spirit continues to thrive.

Understanding our LGA's basic facts matters, especially as the diaspora seeks to stay connected to home. Whether it's tracking development, understanding our people's demographic shifts, or simply holding onto geographical reference points that anchor our identity, these details matter. Essien Udim's land area and population figures tell the story of a community that continues to grow and evolve, even as many of us build lives elsewhere.

For the diaspora community, reconnecting with the specifics of our home—its size, its people, its character—is part of maintaining the bonds that tie us to Akwa Ibom, regardless of the miles that separate us.
