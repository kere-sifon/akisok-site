Mkpat Enin Local Government Area sits in Akwa Ibom's southern reaches, anchored by its administrative hub in Mkpat Enin town. The LGA spans roughly 397 square kilometers of territory that's surprisingly rich in natural wealth. Beyond the oil and gas that have long defined the region's economy, Mkpat Enin also holds deposits of limestone, magnesium, and gypsum—minerals that speak to the area's geological fortune and untapped potential.
