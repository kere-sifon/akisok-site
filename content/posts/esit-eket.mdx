Esit Eket holds a distinctive place in Akwa Ibom's geography as both a significant town and a fully-fledged Local Government Area. For those of us from the diaspora, it's worth knowing that Uquo serves as the administrative nerve center of this LGA, anchoring governance and public services for the communities within Esit Eket. Positioned squarely within Akwa Ibom South Senatorial Zone, this region represents part of the state's southern expanse and the rich tapestry of local administration that sustains our homeland. Like many of Akwa Ibom's 31 local government areas, Esit Eket carries its own historical weight and contemporary significance—a reminder of how our state is organized at the grassroots level.
