Home-grown tech is reshaping how we shop and do business in Akwa Ibom. Cecula, launched in 2022 by innovator Akaninyene Archibong, represents the kind of entrepreneurial energy that's putting our state on the map in Nigeria's tech landscape. What makes IBOMSTORE particularly compelling is its ambition to go beyond the typical e-commerce experience—the platform is built with a thoughtful eye toward what local customers actually need, combining a diverse marketplace with intentional design that prioritizes user experience.

For those of us in the diaspora, platforms like this matter in ways that go beyond transactions. They're evidence of the infrastructure and talent emerging right here at home. The founding of Cecula just a couple of years ago signals that Uyo is becoming a hub where young founders are taking bold risks, learning as they grow, and building products that address real gaps in the market. That's something to watch and to support.

Whether you're thinking about investing, collaborating, or simply staying connected to the innovation happening in Akwa Ibom, knowing about these ventures keeps you plugged into the momentum. The question isn't just what IBOMSTORE does—it's about what it represents: the possibility of building something meaningful from Uyo itself.
