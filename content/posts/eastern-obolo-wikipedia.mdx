Eastern Obolo holds a distinctive place in Akwa Ibom's geography and history. As one of the state's coastal local government areas, it emerged as its own administrative entity in 1996, carved out from the former Ikot Abasi LGA. The LGA's administrative center sits in Okoroete, a town that serves as the heart of governance for the area.

With a population exceeding 76,500 residents, Eastern Obolo represents a significant community within our state's demographic landscape. The LGA's coastal location has historically shaped its character, economy, and cultural identity, making it distinct from the inland areas of Akwa Ibom.
